# Tenses for daily work

- Present Simple vs Present Continuous - describing your routine and current tasks in a stand-up
- Past Simple - reporting what you did yesterday
- Articles a, an, the - talking about servers, bugs and features
- Present Perfect vs Past Simple - writing status updates ("I have fixed the bug")
- Present Perfect Continuous - explaining long tasks ("I have been debugging this for two hours")
- Past Continuous and Past Simple - describing how a production bug happened
- Future forms: will, going to, Present Continuous - planning sprint tasks
- Countable and uncountable nouns (data, information, feedback, much, many) - talking about datasets
