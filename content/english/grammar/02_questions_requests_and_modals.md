# Questions, requests and modals

- Question word order - asking clear questions in team chat
- Polite requests: could you, would you mind, can I - asking teammates for help
- Modals of ability and possibility: can, could, may, might - discussing possible solutions
- Modals of obligation and advice: must, have to, should - writing code review comments
- Modals of deduction: must be, might be, can't be - guessing the cause of a bug
