# Natural and professional English

- Gerunds and infinitives - making suggestions ("I suggest using", "We decided to migrate")
- Dependent prepositions (depend on, responsible for, interested in) - describing your role and tasks
- Phrasal verbs for developers: set up, roll back, look into, figure out
- Indirect questions - asking politely ("Could you tell me why the build failed?")
- Reported speech - summarizing meetings and client requests
- Third Conditional - writing post-mortems ("If we had added monitoring, we would have noticed it")
- Future Continuous and Future Perfect - deadlines and delivery estimates
